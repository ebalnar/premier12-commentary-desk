📘 Premier12 Rules

Broadcast reference compiled from WBSC Premier12 Tournament Regulations (24 August 2026), the 2025–26 WBSC Rules Differences sheet, and the existing two-page broadcast quick reference.

Game length

Nine innings. Run-ahead rule: 15 runs after five innings or 10 runs after seven, once the trailing team has completed its required innings.

Extra innings

Each extra inning begins with runners on first AND second. Not MLB’s runner-on-second-only format.

Pitchers

WBSC rules require a pitcher to face at least one batter, not MLB’s three-batter minimum. The supplied tournament regulations do not specify a numerical pitch-count limit or mandatory rest schedule.

Pitch clock

WBSC comparison: 20 seconds with bases empty. Confirm actual implementation, timing with runners aboard and enforcement with tournament officials.

Mound visits

Three free defensive trips to the mound (WBSC Rule 13.11).

Fifth-inning break

Up to five minutes for infield cleanup after the bottom of the fifth; confirm timing with the technical meeting.

Qualification

Four teams play a single round robin (three games each). The top two qualify for the 2027 Premier12. No Super Round at this qualifier.

Tiebreakers

Head-to-head among tied teams, then TQB, earned-run TQB, batting average, C11.3 procedure, world ranking, coin flip. TQB = (runs scored / offensive innings) − (runs allowed / defensive innings), among tied teams.

Offensive conferences

Three per regulation game. Unused conferences do not carry into extra innings; one additional conference per three extra innings.

Roster requirements

24–26 players; at least 10 pitchers and two catchers.

Lineups & starters

Day-one starters announced at pre-tournament technical meeting. Subsequent starters submitted within one hour of previous day’s final game; confirm official lineup cards.

MLB differences

No MLB-style defensive shift restrictions; collision penalties can include disqualification; WBSC appeals and review differ from MLB.

Replay / ABS / bases

Confirm replay challenges and reviewable calls with officials. Do not assume ABS at the 2026 qualifier. 18-inch bases are subject to WBSC approval.

Confirm with the technical commissioner

Pitch clock activation and runners-on timing; any supplementary pitcher limits; video-review process; 18-inch bases and ABS; fifth-inning broadcast restart.
